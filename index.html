Why Cybersecurity

I like cybersecurity because it combines logic, pressure, and problem solving. Defending a system means understanding where it can fail, how people actually use it, and what needs to happen to keep it reliable. I’m interested in the technical side of the field, but also in the reasoning behind it: routing paths, access control, configuration choices, weak points, and the decisions that make a system stronger or weaker.
